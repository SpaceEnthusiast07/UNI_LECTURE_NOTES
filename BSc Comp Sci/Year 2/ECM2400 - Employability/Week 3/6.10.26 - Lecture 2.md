#### Cover Letter
You want to answer 3 particular questions:
 - Why the company? ~20%
 - Why the role? ~20%
 - Why you? ~60%

Always end with a concluding sentence and sign off (yours sincerely, etc)

#### Speculative Applications
A speculative application is an application to a company who aren't advertising a role, requiring you to contact them directly and ask.

Typically, large companies always advertise if they are offering a placement, so speculative applications are mainly made to SMEs (Small to Medium sized Enterprises).


#### Psychometric Tests
These tests aren't technical; they test who you are as a person, how you would deal with situations, and how you match the role.

They involve pattern recognition tests, scenario based tests, numerical tests, etc.


#### Video Interviews
Contrary to the name, video interviews are *not with actual people*, they simply have *questions popping up on the screen*, then you have a certain amount of time to think about them, then you *record your answer*.
