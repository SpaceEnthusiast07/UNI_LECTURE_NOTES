#### About this Module
You are not expected to read extra material outside of lectures/workshops/exercises.

Post questions on the ELE Forum for real-time updates to all students!


#### Structure
Lectures - Teaches you theory
Workshops - Teaches you practical


#### Assessments
**Coursework** - worth 40% and consists of 4 questions that assess your practical skills.

**Exam** - worth 60% and has the same structure of 4 questions that link to the 4 topics during the term.


#### Coursework
Each question is designed for a specific week

"Workshop" label means the question is very similar to questions covered in the workshops.
"Transfer" label means you need to do a bit of research.

All questions are answered using Linux.

Contains a bit of "Pen" testing (penetration).
