
#### Key Cornerstones of Cryptography
**Confidentiality**
Ensuring data (objects) are *only accessible by authorised parties* (subjects).

**Integrity**
Ensuring the data has *not been manipulated* in between transmission and receiving.

**Authenticity**
Is the person (subject) who sent the data the person they say they are.
