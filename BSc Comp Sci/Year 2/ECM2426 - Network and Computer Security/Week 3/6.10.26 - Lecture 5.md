
#### Stream vs Block Ciphers
**Stream Ciphers**
These are ciphers that *encrypt bit-by-bit* or byte-by-byte, i.e. as data comes in, it is encrypted there and then.
They generally use the XOR operation to encrypt the stream using a keystream.

**Block Ciphers**
These *encrypt blocks of data*, such as 64-bits for DES and 128-bits or AES.
They use a secret key to encrypt each block; the key may change or remain the same between each block.

#### Data Encryption Standard

DES works by using 16 rounds of Feistel cipher!

DES is not secure by modern standards. Therefore, Triple-DES was created, which simply performs DES 3 times, and is now secure.


#### Advanced Encryption Standard
AES replaced DES as the modern block cipher.

AES is a symmetric cipher (i.e. the same key is used to encrypt as to decrypt).

AES uses 128-bit blocks rather than 64-bits in DES. It can also use different size keys: 128, 192, and 256.

AES has modes of operation, one is more secure than the other.
