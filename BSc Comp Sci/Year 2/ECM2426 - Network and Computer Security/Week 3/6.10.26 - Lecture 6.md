
#### Public Key Encryption
A **key generation function** generates a public (to encrypt) and private (to decrypt) key.

Since the **public key** is used to *encrypt*, it can be shared with anyone because it cannot be used to decrypt.

Then, the **private key** must be kept secret as it can *decrypt* the message.


#### RSA
RSA stands for the surnames of the 3 men who created it: Ron Rivest, Adi Shamir, and Leonard Adleman, in 1978.
