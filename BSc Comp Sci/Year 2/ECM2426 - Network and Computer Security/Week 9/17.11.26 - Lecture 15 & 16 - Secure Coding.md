#### A01: Broken Access Control
Def: An actor can act outside their intended permissions.

Key Points:
 - Authorise on the server - Always check protected actions on the server, not the client side.
 - Deny by default - Only grant permissions that are explicitly required.
 - Scope data access - Limit read and write access to records the actor is allowed to access.
