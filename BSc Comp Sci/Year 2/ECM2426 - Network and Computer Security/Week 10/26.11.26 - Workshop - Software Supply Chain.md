
#### Software Supply Chain
A software supply chain refers to the chain of dependencies a project has; each dependency is either direct or transitive.

To start off, a **dependency** is a *library* or external class that your *software relies on* to perform its function.

**Direct dependencies** are dependencies that *your software imports and uses directly*. On the other hand, *transitive dependencies* are dependencies *used by your direct dependencies*, but not directly by your software.
