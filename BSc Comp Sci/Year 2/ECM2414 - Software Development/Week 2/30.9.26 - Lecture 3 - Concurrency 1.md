
#### What is Concurrency?
**Concurrency** is about the *structure of your program*, specifically a program that can be *broken down into smaller processes (threads)*.

The job of the *compiler* is to *determine* whether the program's threads are *run sequentially or in parallel*.

The *Operating System* then *determines* whether these threads will run on the *same core or across multiple cores*.

**Sequential processing** is where each task (threads or processes) is fully completed before moving onto the next task (threads or processes).

**Parallel processing** is where two or more tasks (threads or processes) are executed simultaneously, either on the same core (using round robin or something) or across multiple cores.


#### What are Threads?
**Threads** are smaller, lightweight *baby processes* within a bigger main process (program).

We use threads rather than separate programs because threads share the same main memory space and can have their own isolated memory space.
