
#### Converting ER to Relational Model
The aim of this lecture is to teach you how to convert the entity-relationship model in the conceptual design to a relational model in the logical design.

There are lots of cases to cover when converting between models. They are explained below.


#### Converting Entities
As defined in previous lectures, a strong entity is an entity that isn't reliant on the existence of another entity.

To convert a strong entity, simply *create a relation (table)* with the *same name as the entity* with *all the base attributes*. Keep in mind that you only keep the base attributes when converting composite attributes. Below is an example:
![[Pasted image 20261015115256.png|199]]
Converts to:
```SQL
AcademicStaff(staffID, staffFName, staffLName, academicPosition, gener, DOB)
Primary Key: staffID
```
Notice the removal of `staffName` and the retention of `staffFName` and `staffLName`.
