```SQL_Relational_Model
Person(driverID,fName,lName,address,referedBy)
Primary Key: driverID
Foreign Key: referedBy references Person(driverID)

Car(regNo,model,year,ownerID)
Primary Key: regNo
Foreign Key: ownerID references Person(driverID)

Accident(reportNo,location,date,detail)
Primary Key: reportNo

AccidentParticipation(accidentID,driverID,regNo,damageAmount)
Primary Key: accidentID,driverID,regNo
Foreign Key: accidentID references Accident(reportNo)
Foreign Key: driverID references Person(driverID)
Foreign Key: regNo references Car(regNo)
```
