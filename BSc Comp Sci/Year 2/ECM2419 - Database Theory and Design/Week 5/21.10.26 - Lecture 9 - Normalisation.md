
#### Normalisation
**Normalisation** is a method used to *enhance* the relational model.

It is a process that allows us to *produce a set of relations*, in the relational model, *with desired properties*.

The properties are to remove redundancy, and the potential or insertion, modification, and deletion anomalies.

**Insertion** anomalies occur when a new record or tuple is added to the database, but not all information is available to fill in its attributes, leading to null values.

**Modification** anomalies occur when you have duplicate records/tuples in the database and update an attribute in one duplicate, but not the other.

**Deletion** anomalies occur when you have two distinct entities in the same relation/table and need to delete some data (some attributes), but this also deletes all data in the record/tuple (all attributes).
