**3rd Normal Form:**

Property(propertyNo,pAddress)
Primary Key: propertyNo

Staff(staffNo,sName)
Primary Key: staffNo

Car(carReg,milage,motDate)
primaryKey: carReg

Inspection(inspectionNo,iDate,iTime,Comments,propertyNo,staffNo,carReg)
Primary Key: inspectionNo
Foreign Key: propertyNo references Property(propertyNo)
Foreign Key: staffNo references Staff(staffNo)

#### Exercise 2: Transform to 3NF
![[Pasted image 20261102141607.png|375]]
This table is in 1st normal form.

2NF:
Since two or more ships can have the same name, launched on the same day, owned by the same company, or registered in the same place, the simply way to convert this relational instance to 2nd normal form is to add a shipID attribute, meaning the primary key is not a composite key, hence no partial dependencies can exist.

Ship(shipID, shipName, Registered, LaunchDate, Owner, GrossRegisteredTonnage, CaptainName, CaptainStartDate, Captain)
