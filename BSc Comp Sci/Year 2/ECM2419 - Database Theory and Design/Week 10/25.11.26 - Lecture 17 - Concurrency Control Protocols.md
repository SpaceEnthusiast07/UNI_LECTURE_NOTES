
#### What is Concurrency Control
Remember from the software development module in the first few weeks that covered concurrency.

We learnt that concurrency is a idea or method to structuring a program so you can split it up into individual tasks, called threads, that can be executed sequentially or in parallel.

In database theory, **concurrency control** refers to the measures put in place to ensure parallel transactions always produce the same result no matter the schedule used, and never use dirty data (data that is read, but written to by another transaction in the mean time).
